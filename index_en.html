How to Play?

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries. The color of the boxes will change to give you clues.

A
Green: Correct The letter is in the word and in the right place.
B
Yellow: Another Position The letter is in the word, but in another position.
C
Red: Not Included The letter is not in the hidden word.
💡 Progressive Clues: You will get a clue on the 5th try and the full description on the finish.
